Statement identity, Challenge vs Solution (5 theorems)
  Solution.lean is generated from Challenge.lean by scripts/make_solution.py: it copies the text of
    Challenge.lean from `set_option autoImplicit false` on and replaces each of the five placeholders
    by one `exact`. The script asserts that restoring the placeholders gives the Challenge text back,
    that the block between `-- BEGIN SHARED DEFINITIONS` and `-- END SHARED DEFINITIONS` equals the
    same block of HermiteCompositum/Defs.lean byte for byte, and that each declaration of the block
    between `-- BEGIN HEIGHT DEFINITIONS` and `-- END HEIGHT DEFINITIONS` occurs verbatim in
    HeightT2/Defs.lean. Solution.lean does not repeat the definitions: it reaches the modules that
    declare them (HermiteCompositum.Defs, HeightT2.Defs) through the proof modules it imports, and the Comparator checks that the
    declarations reached from the statements are the same in both environments.
